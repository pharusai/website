You sold it. Did you have it?

Most back-office disasters start with a good sales day. Pharus keeps one ledger of what you have, what's committed and what's on the way, and checks every order and plan against it the moment it lands.

01

The $5,000 air-freight bill

A production run came up short on pouches nobody reordered. Fix: $5,000 in air freight. Pharus watches materials against the production calendar and drafts the PO at the vendor's lead time.

02

The campaign with no stock behind it

A brand built its biggest Black Friday campaign on inventory that wasn't landing in time. Pharus checks plans and ad spend against replenishment dates and flags the gap before the spend goes out.

03

The order that ships short

A PO lands. Pharus checks it against on-hand, committed and inbound before drafting the order, and flags the shortfall the morning it arrives, not the week it ships.

Before you hand anything over

Fair questions.

Is this another system I have to manage?

No. It works with the tools you already use. You approve its work from email or Slack.

Will it do anything without asking me?

No. Pharus drafts; you approve. Every order, PO, payment match and journal entry waits for your tap, with the source document attached. If you later want routine items to post on their own, that's a setting you turn on.

Does it need access to my email?

Only the folders and senders you choose. It reads POs, invoices and remittances. It never sends email as you.

How long does setup take?

Usually within your first week. We set it up with you from your real data and stay through the first live orders.

Does it work with my 3PL?

By API, EDI or their daily reports. Tell us who you use and we'll confirm what's live. [LIST LIVE 3PL CONNECTORS]

Can I use it from Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Pharus works directly with Claude and Codex: ask about stock, orders or margins and post work back.
