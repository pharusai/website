Is this another system my team has to manage?

No. Pharus works with the tools you already use, including directly with Claude and Codex, and does the routine work itself. You review its work; you don't feed it forms.

What happens when Pharus isn't sure?

It asks one precise question with a recommended default and the original document attached. It never silently guesses on money, and you choose which actions always require review.

What can Pharus see in my email?

Only the folders and senders you choose. It reads POs, invoices, and remittances into work. It never sends email as you, and you can remove access at any time.

How long does setup take?

Under 48 hours for most brands. Connect your tools, and Pharus builds your inventory and order picture from your real data. We stay with you through the first live orders at every tier.
